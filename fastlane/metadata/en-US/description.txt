Neon Fold is a focused puzzle game built around the idea of folding the playfield like a sheet of paper. Each fold moves one half of the grid onto the other, and your goal is to bring matching neon glyphs together by choosing the correct sequence of folds within the limits of each stage. Study the glowing symbols, fold the sheet left, right, up, or down using buttons or swipe controls, and preview the result of each move before committing. Visual cues help you understand how folds will align glyphs, move them across the grid, or create mismatches. If a sequence goes wrong, you can undo or reset your moves and try again. Progress through handcrafted stages that gradually increase in complexity, with 2x2, 3x3, and 4x4 sheets offering increasingly challenging spatial puzzles. Enjoy smooth folding animations, realistic crease lines, paper movement, and a minimalist neon visual style featuring cyan, magenta, and gold glyphs. Track your progress with high scores and career statistics, personalize your player profile with a name and portrait, and adjust haptics, sound, fold hints, animations, and glyph glow. Designed for short, focused sessions, Neon Fold combines spatial reasoning, careful planning, and clean visual presentation without timers or unnecessary distractions
